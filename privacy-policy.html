Downloader Pro Integration is a companion for the Downloader Pro desktop application. This policy explains exactly what the extension accesses and what it does with it. The extension does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any of your data to the developer or any third party. Everything it reads stays on your own machine.

What the extension accesses, and why

  • The current tab’s address and page media requests — to detect downloadable files, videos, and audio on the page you’re viewing so you can send them to the desktop app.
  • Cookies & request headers for a download you start — passed to the desktop app so a file behind a login downloads correctly, exactly as it would in your browser. Read only for a download you initiate, sent only to the app on your own machine.
  • Browser downloads — to optionally hand a download you click to the desktop app instead of the browser’s built-in downloader.

Where your data goes

All communication happens over the local loopback address (127.0.0.1) on your own computer, between this extension and the Downloader Pro desktop app. No data is ever sent to the developer, to any website, or to any remote server. The extension contacts no external services of its own.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, log, or store your browsing history.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or fingerprinting.
  • We do not sell or share any information with anyone.
  • We do not transmit cookies or credentials anywhere other than the desktop app on your own machine.

Data retention

The extension keeps only a small, temporary, in-memory list of media URLs seen on the current tab (to fill the download menu). It’s cleared when the tab is closed or reloaded. The developer retains no data of any kind.

Permissions

The extension requests broad host access (<all_urls>) only so it can detect downloadable media on whatever site you choose to use it on. It acts only when you invoke it — a click, the context menu, or a download — and runs no background activity that reports anywhere.
